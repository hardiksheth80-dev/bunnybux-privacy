Request account & data deletion

BunnyBux is a family screen-time, activity and rewards app. Parents or other account holders may ask us to delete their account and associated personal information. An authorized parent may also request deletion of a child's profile.

How to make a request

  1. From the app: Sign in as the Main Parent, open Family Profile and use Request account deletion (when available in your installed version).
  2. Without the app: Email the BunnyBux privacy contact using the button below. Use the email address associated with your BunnyBux account, if possible. State whether you want your entire family account deleted or a particular child's profile removed.
  3. We will verify account ownership or parental authority before acting. Do not send a PIN, password, identity document, or child's sensitive health information in your first message.

Email a deletion request

If the email button does not open your email application, send your request directly to jane.shah81@gmail.com.

What deletion covers

Depending on the request and your relationship to the family, deletion may include parent account information, child profiles, family setup information, screen-time rules, activity and reward records, device associations and other personal data connected with the account.

We may need to retain limited information where required by law, to complete an outstanding request, prevent abuse or resolve disputes. We will explain any applicable retention when responding to your verified request.

Important details

  • Unlinking a device does not itself delete a BunnyBux account or cloud data.
  • A request does not necessarily cause immediate deletion; we must verify authorization and process the related records securely.
  • If you request deletion of a whole family account, other family members may lose access to shared records and rewards.
  • If you need only a child's profile removed, please specify that rather than requesting deletion of the full family account.
