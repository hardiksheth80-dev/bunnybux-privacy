BunnyBux Privacy Policy Effective Date: 23 October 2026 BunnyBux is a family-focused application that helps parents and guardians manage children's screen time, track activities, establish reward rules and manage family-connected devices. This Privacy Policy explains how BunnyBux collects, uses, shares, stores and protects information when parents, guardians and children use the application. 1. Information We Collect Depending on the features enabled, BunnyBux may process: • Account and family information: Parent or guardian account details, child profiles, family relationships, login credentials or authentication identifiers, and authorized caregiver permissions. • Device information: Device identifiers, operating system information, connected-device details, permissions and device-pairing status. • Screen-time information: Screen-time requests, parental approvals, selected applications for control, unlock durations, restrictions and usage-related records. • Health and activity information: Steps, sleep duration, calories burned, physical activities and related fitness data, where users authorize compatible devices or services to share this information. • Rewards information: Earned rewards, activities submitted for approval, screen-time credits, reward-wallet balances and related transactions. • Technical information: Information reasonably necessary for application operation, troubleshooting, security and preventing unauthorized access. The information processed depends on the type of account, connected devices, permissions granted and available application features. 2. How We Use Information BunnyBux processes information to: • Establish and manage connected family accounts. • Enable parents and guardians to approve activities and screen-time requests. • Apply parent-selected application restrictions and authorized temporary unlocks. • Track and display activities, rewards and relevant health or fitness information. • Maintain reward balances and transaction histories. • Synchronize information between authorized family devices. • Protect accounts, investigate technical issues and maintain service reliability. 3. Children's Information BunnyBux is designed for family use under parental or guardian supervision. Parents and authorized guardians may create or manage children's profiles and determine which activities, rewards and device information are shared within the family. Children's information is intended to be accessible only to authorized family members and service providers necessary to operate the application, subject to the application's actual permissions and safeguards. 4. Android Accessibility Service BunnyBux uses Android's Accessibility Service for parental screen-time management on supported Android devices. When enabled, the service can identify the application currently open on the device. If that application has been selected for restriction and the applicable screen-time allowance has expired, BunnyBux can return the device to its home screen. The Accessibility Service is configured not to retrieve the contents of other applications' windows. Users receive an in-app explanation and an opportunity to consent before enabling the service through Android Settings. The service can be disabled through Android Settings. 5. Health and Activity Data Where supported and authorized, BunnyBux may access information such as steps, sleep duration, calories burned and physical-activity records to help families track activity and administer rewards. Access depends on device capabilities, operating-system permissions and the settings selected by users. Such information is intended for family activity and rewards features, not for medical diagnosis or treatment. 6. Sharing of Information Information may be made available to authorized parents, guardians or connected family members according to their roles and permissions. BunnyBux may use technology service providers, including cloud infrastructure, authentication and database providers, to operate and maintain the application. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service. BunnyBux's specific data-sharing practices must correspond to the application implementation and applicable service-provider arrangements. 7. Data Security BunnyBux uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect account information, family records and device-related data. These safeguards may include authenticated access, role-based permissions, secure communications and server-side authorization checks. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. 8. Data Retention and Deletion BunnyBux retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain account functionality and satisfy applicable legal or legitimate operational requirements. Parents and users may request account or associated-data deletion through the account-management mechanisms provided by BunnyBux or the privacy contact identified below. Deletion requests will be handled according to applicable requirements, including deletion of associated personal data, subject to limited legally permissible retention. 9. Parental Controls and Consent Parents and guardians are responsible for authorizing children's participation and determining appropriate screen-time rules and family permissions. Users may withdraw certain device permissions through their device settings. Disabling a permission may affect the associated functionality. 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy BunnyBux may update this policy to reflect application changes, new features, applicable legal requirements or changes in data practices. The updated version will be published with a revised effective date. 11.
